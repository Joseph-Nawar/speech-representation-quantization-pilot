# P0 closest-work audit

Reviewed 2026-11-22. TASA already demonstrates the central cross-task rank and overlap diagnostic in language models and uses aggregate signals under a bit budget. The paired-speech diagnostic remains a distinct empirical question. G5 remains unassessed for a new method.

| Work | Backbone, intervention, supervision and objectives | Strongest relevant comparison, size or hardware, and empirical limits | Relevance to H2 and H3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yeh et al., 2023 | HuBERT Base with a FastConv frontend. BiT and SqWQ quantization-aware training with LibriSpeech and model distillation. Multiple downstream SUPERB tasks with supervised evaluation heads. Uniform weight and activation precision across variants. | DistillHuBERT at comparable storage and alternative QAT losses. Reports calculated storage and estimated runtime. Very low bits worsen ASR and affect downstream tasks differently. Reusable end-to-end code and license unverified. | Prior evidence of task-dependent compression losses. The experiments do not establish isolated block-rank conflict on paired examples or cost-matched shared allocation. [Paper, §§3–4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.00652) |
| Xu et al., ICASSP 2025 | Fine-tuned wav2vec 2.0 Base and HuBERT Large on LibriSpeech. Joint layer precision search and weight training, supervised CTC and teacher–student KL, with an optional post-quantization fine-tuning pass. Single ASR objective. | Best HuBERT result reports 8.6× lossless compression against FP32. Compares uniform and two-stage mixed precision, reports compression time and WER. No reusable implementation/license verified in the paper. | Removes novelty for mixed-precision speech allocation. Its training and single task leave the frozen-head paired conflict and worst-task allocation unanswered. [Paper, §§III–VI](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.03643v2) |
| You Had One Job, 2025 | Frozen LLMs, layer-level, weight-only PTQ. Task-conditioned hidden states and output sensitivity inform per-task precision. The study evaluates multiple tasks using task-specific allocations. | Compares task-blind weight-only approaches at similar precision and reports hardware throughput and latency. Authors report a reference implementation. Availability/license independently unverified. A per-task configuration can specialize to its calibration distribution. | Strong precedent for task-derived sensitivity and precision. The distinct shared-config question is simultaneous worst-task retention. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.06516) |
| Wang et al., TASA, July 2026 | LLaMA-3-8B and Qwen2.5-7B. Training-free task-aware PTQ. Searches a general/task calibration mixture with an activation-energy proxy, profiles isolated layers, combines perplexity and labeled math scores, solves a bit-budget ILP, and refines allocation within layers. ARC is profiled and evaluated separately. | Compares uniform GPTQ/AWQ and mixed-bit alternatives plus calibration/allocation ablations. Simulated weight quantization on A100 and average-bit budgets. Reports precision inversion at 3.5 bits. Task-only calibration can underperform, rank correlations vary by backbone/task. Paper links code, but the public URL returned 404 during audit, leaving code/license unverified. | Direct H2 precedent. Appendix B.4 reports Spearman and B.5 top-K overlap between task signals. H3 has a close additive multi-objective comparator. TASA's weighted aggregate leaves this pilot's normalized worst-task objective on the same speech recordings untested. A generic task-aware aggregation contribution would duplicate existing work. [Paper, §§3–5 and Appendix B](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.00908v1) |
| Li et al., TASLP, August 2026 | Conformer QACT jointly trains 1/2-bit subnetworks. CSVQ applies shared hierarchical vector codebooks at PTQ for 2/3/4-bit modes. Evaluates Conformer, wav2vec 2.0, HuBERT and Whisper on ASR. | Reports 12.86% WER for 2-bit Conformer on Switchboard and gains over scalar PTQ on HuBERT and Whisper. Multiple precision modes share storage. Publisher abstract was accessible. Full text, hardware evidence and code license remain unverified. | Strong current speech PTQ comparator for later method work. ASR-only evidence in the available source leaves H2 and the multi-task H3 test open. [Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1109/TASLPRO.2026.3724410) |
| Borowiecki et al., September 2026 | Nine LLMs across four architecture families. Causal 4→8-bit protect-one and selected pair interventions, inverse single-layer damage, and joint comparisons across knowledge and reasoning tasks. Weight-only PTQ with labeled task scoring. | Contrasts local higher-precision protection with global smaller-group or stronger quantization at matched effective bits per weight. Global granularity wins in their comparisons. Quantization sensitivity can be diffuse and proxy rankings fail to localize recoverable damage. Code/license unverified. | Especially close to the motivation for H3. Establishes a matched-budget comparison between several joint configurations, with different backbones, interventions and aggregate objective from the registered speech test. [Paper, §§3–6 and Appendices E–G](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.01587) |
| Gubian et al., 2025 | Frozen multilingual wav2vec 2.0 with supervised phonetic, tonal and speaker probes, plus representational geometry. No model-weight quantization. | Matched and unmatched pretraining languages and layerwise probes. Reports largely orthogonal subspaces with differing layer trends and a late-layer anomaly. Compression size/hardware inapplicable. Code/license unverified. | Evidence for layerwise speech information differences. Clean probe separability and sensitivity to a weight perturbation are different empirical questions. [Paper, §§3–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.10855) |
| DISSC, 2023 | HuBERT units and a disentangled speech model. Parallel VCTK prompts are used for controlled voice-conversion evaluation. Unit discretization rather than encoder-weight PTQ. | Held-out paired utterance evaluation, with voice-conversion/content metrics and reported limitations in VCTK speaking style. Weight budgets inapplicable. | Prior use of paired VCTK speech to separate speaker and content. The pilot's use of paired recordings itself supplies no method novelty. [Paper](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.541/) |

Targeted checks covered shared multi-task PTQ, speech SSL content and speaker probes, controlled VCTK pairings and isolated versus joint quantization. Two further related results are [Why Does Post-Training Quantization Work?](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.11716) on residual-error interaction, and [Merge-Friendly Post-Training Quantization](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/shin25f.html) on merging target-specific quantized models. Neither is an exact match for the registered paired evaluation. The search found no verified exact reproduction of that evaluation. These findings support review of G5 only after the scientific gates are evaluated.
